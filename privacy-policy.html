Privacy Policy

Money Manager Expense Tracker · Last updated July 16, 2026

Money Manager Expense Tracker (the "App") is a personal expense tracking app developed for individual use. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the App.

1. Data you enter

All expense entries you create (item, amount, category, date, notes, etc.) are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us, stored on any server, or shared with any third party. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted.

2. Advertising

The App displays ads served through Google AdMob. To provide and personalize ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device information (device type, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • App interaction data (ad clicks and impressions)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. We do not control how Google uses this data beyond ad delivery. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings > Google > Ads, on most Android devices).

3. Information we do not collect

The App does not require account creation, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other directly identifying personal information.

4. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over deleting it at any time:

  • Delete individual entries: Open any entry in the App and delete it directly.
  • Delete all data at once: Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Reset App, and confirm by typing "RESET." This permanently erases all entries and resets Categories/Groups to default.
  • Uninstall the App: Removing the App from your device also permanently deletes all locally stored data.

No data is retained by us after deletion, as we never receive or store your entries in the first place. The only data processed outside your device is limited advertising data collected by Google AdMob (see the Advertising section above), which is governed by Google's own retention policies.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to support@oinkoinklabs.com.
